Terms of Service

Keeping it simple — here's how things work.

The queue

  • Remember that you are in a line of commissions.
  • I will not work on weekends so the days didn't count.
  • Just ask me if you have question or something in mind.

Your file

  • I will not start the project until I check the file and say it can be used for rigging.
  • If you're not going to fix the file, I'm going to rig it as it is — so please don't complain.
  • I will let you know twice if the file needs to be fixed. You can fix it back to your artist, or by me with additional fees.

Payment & refunds

  • If I know you or have ever heard about you, that would be 50% payment after I send the first WIP and full payment after I send you the finished project. (Sometimes I forget to send the WIP and send the finished file instead, so please keep that in mind.)
  • If I don't know you or have never heard about you, that would be 50% up-front payment before I start the project.
  • I do not provide refunds after payment has been made, for whatever reason. Please read this carefully.
  • If I am unable to complete or continue the project for any reason, only then will I issue a refund.
  • If payment has been made, that means you're willing to follow and obey my Terms of Service.

Credit & usage

  • I have the right to use your commission as samples. I will only use them for this purpose and upload it right away. (However, if you do not want me to, please let me know.)
  • I do not give the .cmo3 file — if you want it, you can buy it for 150% of the rigging price.
  • I have the right to refuse a project for any reason.
  • I am given full credit as Ryza with a link to my Twitter.
  • Selling my model rigging is not allowed.
